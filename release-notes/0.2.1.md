- **Screenshots are checked on the picture, not the whole screen.** When you share a screenshot of a post, Genned now finds the photo inside it and checks only that, leaving out the status bar, captions and buttons. Before, that surrounding app UI pushed clearly AI images toward "uncertain": on social-app-style screenshots of the hardest benchmark, 25% of AI images were shown as "likely AI"; now 37%, about the same as checking the image itself. Real photos shown as "likely AI" stay at 3% or less.
- Photos straight from a camera (with camera EXIF data) are never cropped.
- **Measured on real phone photos.** A new benchmark of 300 real smartphone photos (iPhone, Samsung, Huawei, Xiaomi, Oppo) shows 3% read as "likely AI" (at most 4.7% after everyday edits), within the app's 5% limit. Many real phone photos still land in "uncertain"; that needs model training, not a threshold change.
