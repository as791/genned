- **Fewer "Uncertain" results on real phone photos.** Both photo detectors were retrained with real phone photos. In our tests, about a third of real phone photos now come out "Uncertain"; before, it was more than half. Real photos wrongly shown as "Likely AI-generated" stayed under 1%.
- **Catches more images from newer AI tools.** On a test set of recent generators, 56% of AI images are now shown as "Likely AI-generated", up from 47%.
- **The "Likely not AI-generated" cut-off for photos is 15% again** (it was 20%). With the new detectors this keeps AI images wrongly shown as "Likely not AI-generated" under 10% on unedited images.
- **Harder to fool on purpose, but not robust.** Some deliberate attacks succeed less often than before. Someone who sets out to fool the app can still do it.
- **Video is unchanged.** The retrained detectors were not better on video, so video keeps the previous ones.
- **Bigger download.** Photos and video now use separate detector files, so each APK is about 33 MB larger (`arm64-v8a` is about 190 MB).
- **Still fully offline.** The detectors ship inside the app; nothing is downloaded or uploaded.
- **Known limits.** Edited AI images (grain, sharpening, screenshots of screenshots) are still the weakest case: up to 13% are shown as "Likely not AI-generated".
