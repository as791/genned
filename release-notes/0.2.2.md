- **Fewer "uncertain" results on photos.** The cut-off for "likely real" on photos and screen captures moves from 15% to 20%. On a benchmark of real phone photos, 44% now get a clear "likely real" answer (was 36%), and "uncertain" drops from 61% to 53%. Real photos shown as "likely AI" are unchanged (about 3%).
- **The cost.** On unedited AI images, at most about 9% now read "likely real" (was 7%). After edits such as film grain, sharpening or screenshot-and-repost, that rises to as much as 20% (was 15%). Treat "likely real" as weaker evidence than "likely AI".
- **Video is unchanged.** Videos keep the 15% cut-off, because at 20% too many AI videos would read "likely real".
