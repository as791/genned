- **Better at catching newer AI photos.** Photos are now checked with a copy of the Community Forensics model fine-tuned on newer open-weights generators and everyday edits. On the harder photo benchmark, 45% of AI images are caught at a 5% false-alarm rate, up from 34%. On 2025–26 generators (OpenFake), AUC rose from 0.86 to 0.93.
- **Fewer false accusations after re-compression.** Real photos saved as WebP were shown as "likely AI" 11–15% of the time; now 1–10%.
- **Harder to fool with a shared pattern.** One published pattern used to turn 33% of real photos into "likely AI"; now 0%. Hiding AI images that way works less often (65%, was 75%). Determined attackers can still fool it; see the README.
- **Video is unchanged.** Videos keep the previous model, which is better on video.
- The app is about 44 MB larger, because it carries both model copies.

Numbers and methods: `internal-docs/MODEL.md` "Phase 3" and issue #16.
