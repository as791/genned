- **Fewer crashes on damaged or unusual files.** A picture with broken metadata, a video with an unreadable frame, or a very large picture on a phone short of memory used to close the app. Now the check skips the bad part or says it couldn't check the file.
- **Content Credentials are checked more strictly.** The C2PA library is updated. A specially built file that is valid as two formats at once was wrongly accepted as validly signed; now it isn't. Checked against the official C2PA test files: 49 of 51 give the same answer as the reference tool, and the other 2 differ for known reasons.
- **Detection is unchanged.** Same detectors and the same answer cut-offs as 0.5.0.
- **Newly measured limits.** A real photo with part of it edited by AI is usually missed: more than half come out "Likely not AI-generated". Real photos of young children are wrongly called AI more often than photos of adults (about 5–8% against about 1%). Every measure is published in the project's evaluation scorecard.
- **Still fully offline.** No new permissions; nothing is downloaded or uploaded.
