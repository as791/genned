How it works

Three taps, a few seconds, and an answer you can understand.

Share it to Genned

From WhatsApp, Instagram, your gallery or any app with a Share button. Or open Genned and pick a photo or video.

Genned checks it on your phone

Two AI detectors look at the picture, and Genned reads what the file says about itself: camera details, AI tool tags and signed Content Credentials.

You get a plain answer

Likely AI-generated, Uncertain, or Likely not AI-generated, with the reasons behind it.

Three answers. No guesswork dressed up as certainty.

Genned only says "likely AI-generated" at 90% or more, and "likely not" below 15%. In between, it says it can't tell, and why, because that range is where detectors are wrong too often.

Likely AI-generated

The checks point to AI. Think twice before you share or believe it.

Uncertain

Genned can't tell, and says so instead of guessing. Treat it as no answer.

Likely not AI-generated

Nothing points to AI. Still an estimate, not proof.

Made for how you actually scroll

A bubble for Instagram and WhatsApp

Turn on the Genned bubble and tap it while you scroll. It checks what's on your screen without leaving the app.

  • Set up in three steps, with a checklist that moves on by itself.
  • The screen is captured only at the moment you tap.
  • Drag it to the ✕ to turn it off.

Verified Content Credentials

Some cameras, editors and AI tools, ChatGPT among them, sign a record into the file of how it was made. When Genned finds one from a trusted signer, it shows "Verified" and what the signer declared, checked against the official C2PA trust lists, offline.

Photos, screenshots and videos

Check photos, Reels and Shorts up to three minutes long. Share a screenshot and Genned checks only the picture inside it, not the app around it.

Your checks, kept on your phone

Your recent checks are on the home screen, and History groups them under Today, Yesterday and Earlier, with small thumbnails only. Swipe one away to delete it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

What it can't do, yet

This person doesn't exist: the photo was made with a recent AI image model. Genned didn't call it AI. It said "Uncertain". It didn't call it real either.

A profile that looks too perfect, plus "Uncertain", is a reason to be careful, not a reason to relax.
  • Pictures from the newest closed AI tools are the hardest case.
  • Edits like grain, sharpening or screenshots of screenshots can hide AI: up to 13% of edited AI images still come out "Likely not AI-generated".
  • Someone who sets out to fool it on purpose can.
  • Every result is an estimate, not proof.

Questions

Is Genned free?

Yes. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases, and open source.

How do I install it?
  1. On your phone, tap Download for Android ({{APK_MB}} MB). It works on nearly every phone sold in the last several years.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or Files app; allow it, then tap Install.
  3. Not sure your phone is supported? Use the universal APK ({{UNIVERSAL_MB}} MB), which works on every Android phone.
Why isn't it on Google Play?

Not yet. Until then, every release is published on GitHub, signed with the same key, with SHA-256 checksums for each download on the release page.

How do I get updates?

Genned has no internet access, so it can't check for updates itself. Come back here, or use Watch → Custom → Releases on the GitHub repository. Install the new APK over the old one; your history is kept.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, Genned is Android only for now.

Can Genned be wrong?

Yes. It is an estimate, not proof. That's why it says "Uncertain" when it can't tell, and why the accuracy section above lists what it still gets wrong.
