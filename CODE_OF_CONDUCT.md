# Code of Conduct

Genned is built by people with different backgrounds, skills and opinions. We want taking
part to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, email or photos, without
  their permission.
- **Posting images of real people to test or dispute a result without their consent.**
  Use images you have the rights to and that don't identify private individuals.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews)
and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainer, [@as791](https://github.com/as791), through the repository's private reporting
form: **Security → Report a vulnerability** at
https://github.com/as791/genned/security/advisories/new. Write "Code of Conduct" in the
title; it is visible only to the maintainers. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Responses scale with the severity and repetition of the
behaviour.
